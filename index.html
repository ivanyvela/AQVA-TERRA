STRATEGIC PILLARS

An Integrated Scientific & Economic Framework

Addressing global freshwater stress requires closing the gap between subsurface physical reality, low-cost sensor telemetry, and rational capital allocation.

01

Subsurface & Satellite Diagnostics

Characterizing aquifers and surface basins using near-surface geophysics and Earth-observation remote sensing.

  • Transient electromagnetics (tTEM) & ERT resistivity tomography
  • Satellite InSAR subsidence & gravimetric groundwater tracking
  • Recharge-zone mapping & transboundary basin modeling
02

Open, Low-Cost Monitoring

Deploying accessible hydrological and geochemical sensor networks for municipalities and underserved communities.

  • Open-hardware piezometric & electrical conductivity nodes
  • Edge-computed telemetry with LoRaWAN & cellular backhaul
  • Community-accessible real-time water quality dashboards
03

Peer-Reviewed Science & Water Economics

Publishing empirical research and economic analyses on global freshwater resilience and cost-effectiveness.

  • Depletion marginal cost curves & water-table valuation
  • Open-access peer-reviewed hydrological monographs
  • Policy advisory for sustainable abstraction & recharge governance
04

Evidence-Backed Water Fund

Directing 100% of net surpluses and philanthropic capital toward rigorously vetted, high-impact clean-water interventions.

  • Scientific due-diligence criteria for capital deployment
  • Quantified cubic-meter impact per invested euro
  • Zero private dividend diversion — 100% impact reinvestment
